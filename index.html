My Philosophy

Mental fitness, through lifestyle.

I believe medication is a tool — not the whole plan. We build mental wellness on the foundations of everyday life, and use medicine with intention: only when it helps, and less of it whenever we can.

01

Lifestyle first

Sleep, movement, nutrition, and connection are the foundation of mental wellness — we start there.

02

Medication with intention

When medication helps, we use the lowest effective dose — never more than you need.

03

Reduce when ready

Deprescribing is a goal, not an afterthought — fewer medications, when it’s right for you.
